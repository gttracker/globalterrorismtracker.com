About GTT

GTT — Global Terrorism Tracker documents and analyzes terrorism and security developments around the world through structured reporting, incident tracking, and analytical coverage.

The platform provides monthly reporting on terrorist incidents and security operations, alongside analytical coverage of major incidents and developments in terrorism and security.

Purpose

GTT aims to provide organized and accessible coverage of terrorism-related developments, bringing together incident reporting and analysis in one platform.

Its work focuses on documenting developments and examining their broader significance through concise and structured reporting and analysis.
